What you need to know

See full T&Cs

  1. To claim your travel cover:

    • To claim with travel cover on Metal: trips must be a maximum duration of 30 days. Delays or disruptions must be 8+ hours to cover your luggage and flights
    • On Ultra, the trip duration should be a maximum of 90 days to be covered. Delays or disruptions must be 4+ hours to cover flights and luggage
    • Trips must be scheduled to start and end at your primary residence
    • In the UK, claims must be made before coverage expires on your 76th birthday
    • Claims related to pre-existing medical conditions are excluded from coverage

  2. To cancel for any reason:

    • All trips and events must be purchased with Revolut at least 72 hours before the departure time
    • The claim must be submitted 24 hours before you were due to leave
    • Trips must be scheduled to start and end at your primary residence
    • Claims related to pre-existing medical conditions are excluded from coverage
