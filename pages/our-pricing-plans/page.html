¹The value shown is an estimate of the average benefits a customer on Plus, Premium, Metal, or Ultra can enjoy over a 12-month period. The value of brand subscriptions is adjusted by country, and the value of embedded insurance and eSIM packages is calculated for the UK. All other benefits represent a combined average across the UK and EEA.

To calculate this, we've added up the standard retail prices of fixed benefits, such as the cost of brand subscriptions, eSIM packages, and airport lounge passes. We then add the savings a customer makes by using a paid plan instead of a free Standard plan. These savings include reduced fees for international transfers, currency exchange, and trading crypto, stocks, or commodities, as well as avoided ATM fees and the extra savings interest or RevPoints customers earn on a higher tier. When calculating these averages, we use data from customers using that specific benefit. The values were calculated based on the period between October 2025 and April 2026.

The value of embedded insurance products (travel, Everyday Protection, or Cancel For Any Reason) is estimated by comparing our coverage, to the average annual cost of similar policies for a typical customer.

For a full breakdown of plan benefits, please refer to our Compare Plans page and Paid Plan Terms.

²Please refer to the policy wording and other related insurance documents for full coverage scope.

³Capital at risk. Fair usage limits apply to all exchanges. This means each month you can exchange Currency, Crypto, and Commodities up to a combined limit of £1,000. If you exceed the limit, we charge an additional fair usage fee of 1% for Standard customers, and 0.5% for Plus customers on any exchange in addition to regular fees or weekend mark-ups.AER stands for Annual Equivalent Rate and illustrates what the interest rate would be if interest was paid and compounded once each year.

⁴For commodities: Capital at risk.

Revolut Ltd's commodities service is not regulated by the FCA and it is not protected or covered by the Financial Ombudsman Service, or the Financial Services Compensation Scheme.

⁵The Annual Equivalent Rate (AER) shows the interest you can earn over 1 year. AER is compounded, so you’ll earn interest on interest already earned. Rates depend on your plan type and savings' currency. Paid plan subscription fees and T&Cs apply. Please refer to the Instant Access Savings T&Cs and our partner T&Cs. For Ultra Plan: Interest is paid at 4% AER (variable) on balances below £200,000, and 3.51% AER (variable) on balances above £200,000. A blended rate applies when your balance exceeds £200,000.
