KRW to GBP currency exchange FAQs

  • Currency exchange rates rise and fall constantly, so it's wise to keep an eye out for current market trends. Our goal is to give you easy access to the most updated info so you can find the best exchange rates for you.

    Before converting South Korean Won to British Pounds, use our currency converter, conversion tables, and price comparison widget to find today's exchange rates and compare different providers. Keep in mind that exchange rates fluctuate constantly, so always double-check our current rate in-app before converting.

    Curious about more than just today's rate? You can also explore the historical exchange rate data for KRW to GBP to see how the value has fluctuated over time.

    Check live KRW to GBP exchange rates now.

  • Depending on your plan, the amount you're exchanging, and the day of the week, you may pay a currency exchange fee. If you're sending KRW to a GBP account, you may also pay a small transfer fee. You'll always see the total cost in-app before you exchange, along with the Revolut exchange rate.

    Currency exchange fees

    If the amount you're exchanging is within your plan’s fair usage limit, you won't pay any additional currency exchange fees on weekdays.

    Depending on your plan, you may pay a fee on any exchanges you make during weekends, between 17:00 Eastern Time (ET) on Fridays and 18:00 ET on Sundays. You'll pay a 1% fee on the Standard plan, and a 0.5% fee on the Plus plan. You won't pay any weekend fees on our Premium, Metal, or Ultra plans.

    If you're over your monthly exchange allowance, you'll pay an additional fair usage fee, depending on your plan (1% for the Standard plan and 0.5% for the Plus plan). There are no fair usage fees with our Premium, Metal, and Ultra plans. These limits are set on a rolling monthly basis. You can check your fair usage limit and the date it will reset under Your plan in your profile.

    Money transfer fees

    When you send KRW to a GBP account, you may pay a small transfer fee. This is the amount we charge to process your transfer. The total cost depends on the transfer method you choose, the destination country, and the amount you're sending, but don't worry — you'll see all costs live in-app before you send. You'll get discounts on these fees with our Premium, Metal, and Ultra plans. Heads up — external banks may charge non-Revolut transfer fees that we can't foresee.

    If you're looking for fee-free money transfers, try a Revolut-to-Revolut transfer. You can send money to other Revolut friends in any of the 36 you hold, without having to convert.

    For more information on your plan's fees, check out our terms and policies. Fees are subject to change, but you'll always see them in-app before you convert.

  • You can convert KRW to GBP with us in 3 ways.

    In-app currency exchange

    You can exchange KRW to GBP and keep both in separate accounts, among 36 supported currencies. Here's how:

    1. Download Revolut for Android or iOS, if you haven't already, and sign up for an account
    2. Add money to your account in KRW
    3. Go to Exchange
    4. Choose KRW as your starting currency and GBP as the currency you want to convert to
    5. Enter the amount you want to convert in KRW or receive in GBP. We'll calculate the conversion for you
    6. Check the exchange rate and any applicable fees
    7. Tap Confirm order and you're all done

    International money transfers

    When you send KRW from South Korea to a GBP account in South Korea, we'll exchange your money in-app and show you our current conversion rate before you send. Here's how to send money:

    1. Download Revolut for Android or iOS, if you haven't already, and sign up for an account
    2. Add money to your account in KRW
    3. Go to Transfers in your Revolut app
    4. Choose your destination country, then enter how much you want to send in KRW or how much you want your recipient to get in GBP
    5. Check our exchange rates, fees, and estimated delivery times in-app
    6. Fill in your recipient's details. You'll usually need their full name, BIC or SWIFT code, and their account number
    7. Tap Send and track your transfer in-app

    Card payments abroad

    When you pay with your Revolut card in South Korea, we'll automatically exchange your money to GBP. Just add money to your account and shop wherever Visa or Mastercard are accepted.
